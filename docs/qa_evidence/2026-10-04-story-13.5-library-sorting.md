## Delivered behavior

- A pure local collection query composes existing lifecycle segments with recent activity, title, or unread updates first. Default organization is Recent with “Recent activity · Newest.”
- Activity supports newest/oldest, title supports A–Z/Z–A, and unread updates has fixed unread-first ordering while retaining read titles. Known activity dates precede unknown dates in both directions; normalized-title and UUID ties are deterministic.
- Organization persists locally through only three new preference keys: segment, sortKey, and sortDirection. Existing density preferences remain intact; Reset restores default organization and preserves density.
- Library controls and summaries expose the selected organization without changing series, chapter, progress, or update metadata. Existing collection/segment empty states remain applicable.
- Source filtering, network behavior, hydration, and Reader zoom are outside this delivery.
