## Interfaces and verified behavior

`ReaderChapterGeometry` is immutable and carries `sessionID`, caller-owned `revision`, and ordered `pageFrames`. Its failable initializer rejects raw nonpositive sizes, nonfinite/unrepresentable extents, vertical overlap and reversed ordering. `anchor(at:)` returns a `ReaderSemanticAnchor` containing session, page index and normalized point. Capture is inclusive at page edges and chooses the lower index on a shared border. `point(for:)` rejects foreign sessions, unavailable indexes and invalid unit points, while allowing the same normalized anchor to project into changed dimensions of the same session.

`ReaderPageMaterializationPolicy.indexes(in:viewport:)` returns sorted desired indexes only. Positive-area two-axis intersection defines visibility; touching an edge is not visible. Visible pages are selected with at most one preceding and one following neighbor. With no visible pages, the nearest vertical page interval wins, lower index breaks ties, and at most one adjacent page is added. At chapter edges an unavailable preferred neighbor falls back to the available opposite neighbor. Empty chapters yield an empty selection; invalid viewport or overflowing distance yields nil. These values do not create native containers or grant progress permission.

Coverage includes invalid raw dimensions and collapsed extents, gaps, foreign anchors, page boundaries, changed dimensions, nine viewport traces, nearest-page ties, variable widths, empty/single-page chapters and five unequal-height visible pages with two neighbors.
