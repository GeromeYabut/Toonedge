## Changed API and coverage

`ReaderStickyBaselineZoomPolicy` is an `Equatable`, `Sendable` value type with read-only displayed scale, committed scale, active-pinch state, and phase. It accepts cumulative magnification through begin/update/end/cancel/reset methods. No schema, repository, loader, view, geometry, or progress API changed.

Prototype bounds remain 0.75x–3x, with downward snap entry at 1.03x and breakthrough after a further inward ratio of 0.88 from engagement. Release commits the valid scale; cancellation restores committed scale; reset clears interaction state. Scale equal to 1 never grants scrolling or progress permission.

Twelve Swift Testing test functions include nine scale-trace arguments and two cancellation arguments. Assertions cover ordinary tracking, exact baseline hold, first-sample overshoot, same-callback excess contraction, equal dense/sparse movement after common engagement, reversal and rearming, bound rebasing, invalid/duplicate samples, extreme finite values, lifecycle no-ops, release, cancellation, and reset.
