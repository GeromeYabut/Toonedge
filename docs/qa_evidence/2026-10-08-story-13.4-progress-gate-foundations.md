## Interfaces and verified behavior

`ReaderAuthorizedReading` captures an immutable input and live authorization. Metadata enrichment may change title and cover but cannot retarget the reading. Effects bind progress, recent reading and cache metadata to their captured inputs; unsupported guarded writers fail closed.

`ReaderProgressGate` owns its coordinator and bounds state to the current observation and operation. Admission requires exact baseline scale, current reconciled geometry, positive-area visibility, actual decoded dimensions, current pipeline/installation, displayed-target availability, and a closed restore window. Superseded operations, accepted readiness loss, inspection, Reset, suspension, geometry changes and terminal retirement revoke prior authority. Foreign or stale observations do not invalidate valid current work.

The guarded UserDefaults writer validates and encodes progress before checking live authorization. Its final check and synchronous mutation share one main-actor turn, with no internal await. It preserves legacy keys and JSON encoding, rejects wrong targets and invalid progress, and reports metadata effects unsupported. Deterministically suspended operations demonstrate rejection at the real backend boundary; valid historical commits remain stored. UserDefaults does not promise immediate disk durability.

Readiness uses owned decoded-image fixtures here. Native observation provenance, actual displayed/hittable targets and real pipeline subscriptions remain later integration requirements.
