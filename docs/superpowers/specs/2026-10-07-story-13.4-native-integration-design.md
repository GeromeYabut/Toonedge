## Scope and user flow

- Keep the existing iPhone-first chapter reader, local progress model, and View Original Page escape hatch.
- Adopt the already implemented pure scale policy without changing its prototype tuning: 0.75x minimum, 3x maximum, 1.03x downward snap entry, and 0.88 further inward ratio after engagement.
- Preserve enlarged and below-baseline scale on release. Returning to 1x restores the frozen reading anchor and resumes ordinary scrolling only after measured reconciliation.
- Support bounded pan, double tap, Reset Zoom, non-pinch accessibility actions, fixed chrome, and Reduce Motion after the native candidate proves feasible.
- Keep one existing image pipeline with maximum three concurrent fetch/decode operations. Do not introduce eager whole-chapter loading, additional pipelines, zoom persistence, or a schema migration.

Open at the existing saved position. Outward pinch enlarges around the inspected content point; release retains that presentation. Inward pinch settles at exactly 100%, then continued inward movement in the same gesture deliberately crosses below it. Release at a non-baseline scale remains in inspection mode. Reset returns to the frozen normal-reading position. At baseline, release alone is insufficient to publish progress: layout and anchor acknowledgement must also be current.

Double tap enlarges idle 1x to 2x around the tap and returns any off-baseline presentation to normal. Consumed pinch, pan, reset, and double tap must not toggle chrome. An accessibility shrink action can explicitly select 0.75x without traversing the pinch detent; enlargement can select 2x. Controls and VoiceOver focus remain usable while reading progress is frozen.

The native candidate should not nest a second chapter-scrolling view. One UIScrollView owns baseline vertical scrolling and inspection pan/zoom; SwiftUI presents it through an adapter. Existing Reader chrome stays outside its transformed canvas. The current production SwiftUI reader remains the default until the complete adoption gate passes.

## Approved container-count resolution

Retain the existing bound of visible page containers plus two. Let V be the count of page rectangles with positive-area intersection with the actual content viewport, after accounting for scale and insets. Merely touching an edge is not visible. A short image chapter can legitimately have several visible pages; this cap is not a fixed maximum of three containers.

For V greater than zero, materialize all visible pages and at most two non-visible neighbors, one before and one after where available. At chapter edges omit missing neighbors; never synthesize additional containers to fill a quota. Recompute selection against the current geometry revision, not old callbacks. The image pipeline independently retains its existing bounded prefetch policy; container count and fetch/decode count are different measures.

For V equal to zero, materialize the nearest page plus at most one adjacent page, never more than two. Choose the nearest page by distance between its interval and the viewport interval; ties choose the lower index. Choose the adjacent page toward the viewport center relative to the nearest page's center; on an exact center tie choose the following page, falling back to the preceding page at the chapter end. Zero-page chapters materialize none. Releasing a reused page clears its image and callbacks before assignment to a new page/session.

This intentionally changes the historical zero-visible nearest-plus-two algorithm while preserving its cap. The user approved this two-container resolution; the alternative cap of three is not approved. No product code is changed by this document. The executable foundation plan clarifies that a missing preferred adjacent page at a chapter edge falls back to the available opposite neighbor; it never fabricates a page or exceeds the cap.

## Anchor and geometry transactions

Capture stable page identity, the normalized point within that page, and its required viewport position when inspection starts. Keep the frozen reading anchor distinct from the current inspected focal point and the last committed inspection presentation. Cancellation restores the committed scale and presentation; Reset restores normal size and the frozen reading anchor. Repeated Reset supersedes older acknowledgements rather than reusing their identity.

Queue decoded-dimension, viewport, spacing, fit-mode, rotation, and inset changes through one ordered reconciliation path. Every snapshot carries session identity, geometry revision, and interaction epoch. Never apply an older geometry revision after a newer accepted one. Measure the native viewport only after its insets/layout are current. A delayed initial restore must not overwrite user-interacted state, and session replacement invalidates all former callbacks.

Progress becomes eligible only when the gesture has ended, actual layout is at baseline, the latest reconciliation is complete, and the frozen anchor is measured against that current geometry. Scale equal to 1 and zero translation are not acknowledgements. Failure to obtain a valid acknowledgement leaves progress suspended; it must not manufacture a timeout-based success. Recovery must keep Reset and View Original Page reachable and show an honest recoverable state rather than strand the reader.

No normalized anchor is added to persistent storage by this scope. The zoom anchor is transient and preserves the current within-page position during inspection. Existing saved progress remains page-index-based unless a separate persistence requirement is approved.

## Current progress call-path audit

The baseline inspected is merged main `3bdb729`. PR 11 adds no integration changes to these paths:

- `ReaderView.swift`, `readerStrip` and `selectVisiblePage`, feeds pipeline visibility and asynchronously calls `markImageVisible`. The stored-position task awaits `restoreProgress`, then scrolls to a page top.
- `ReaderViewModel.swift`, `markImageVisible` and `markImageReady`, chooses the visible ready page and calls `updateProgress`.
- `observePagePipelineReadiness` schedules additional asynchronous `markImageReady` calls independently of view callbacks.
- `updateProgress` changes displayed progress, then saves through the progress repository, enriches metadata, records recent reading, and records recent cache metadata across awaits. Repository effects can drive library/completion state; these paths need spies, not only view assertions.
- `replaceSession` awaits stored progress before `installSession`, and appearance/disappearance swaps or cancels pipelines. Session/restore epochs must fence suspended work and new readiness subscriptions.

An entry guard alone cannot fence an operation that resumes after an await. Revalidate the current interaction/session token before every downstream effect and test delayed saves, delayed enrichment, readiness, restore, recent-reading writes, cache metadata, and completion consequences. Cancellation cannot retract a repository write that already started. The executable plan must resolve commit-boundary semantics using the actual repository implementations and controlled commit spies; if it cannot prove the required isolation, candidate adoption is blocked rather than described as safe. No weaker side-effect exception is approved here.

## Native evidence and acceptance

Use only owned synthetic images with known fiducials. Validate decoded/hittable targets and reachability before measuring; unavailable scenarios are inconclusive. Never store source artwork, cookies, credentials, protected local screenshots, or sensitive URLs in evidence.

The converted semantic anchor must be within 2 points on each axis of its required position. Settled/reset measurements must stay within tolerance for at least one continuous second. This is evidence stability, not a production delay. Retain the existing four-frame-value comparison for an unchanged decoded target. When decoded dimensions change, compare the same normalized point in true new geometry; do not insist on obsolete dimensions. Active geometry transactions must meet the same 2-point focal continuity requirement.

The native matrix includes slow/fast pinch, detent engagement, baseline release, deliberate breakthrough, below-baseline release, bounded pan on both axes, cancellation above/below baseline, double tap, repeated Reset, chrome/Original Page coexistence, and short/long chapters at first/middle/last pages. It also covers spacing/fit/insets/rotation, differing delayed decoded dimensions, late restore, memory pressure, page-local retries, cancellation, chapter/session replacement, stale callbacks, and rapid geometry revisions.

Inspect actual container count/reuse, single scroll ownership, fetch/decode concurrency, image residency, and strict repository commit spies throughout inspection and reset. Progress returning at idle measured baseline must work, not remain permanently disabled. Viewport tests with tablet-sized/narrow-window inputs exercise geometry only; they do not establish iPad support.

Native acceptance requires both dedicated current-installed-runtime iPhone simulators. The known identities are iPhone 16e `4582CDE9-27DB-4669-86AC-0631C1D7F2ED` and iPhone 16 Pro Max `29E33EEE-8A11-457F-8F7F-BDF2D44A9FE4`; reverify availability/runtime before use and request direction if unavailable. Never target shared iPhone 16 Pro `04F65B71-EEB9-4085-BFBD-8B7406E480A2`. Actual VoiceOver and system Reduce Motion are separate required native checks, not accessibility-label or animation-flag substitutes. iOS 17 runtime coverage remains waived; deployment/API availability stays at iOS 17. The app remains iPhone-only.

## Stop and recovery rules

The old research worktree is reference-only and remains untouched. Reuse reviewed concepts only after inspection and adaptation against current main; do not apply frozen corrective patches wholesale or imply that their earlier compilation established native GREEN.

A new experiment records its start and absolute UTC deadline at actual execution. Guards run inside every mutation/test command and again after approval waits or other blocking operations. At the reserved stopping point, stop new work and restore temporary wiring before the deadline; retain safe evidence and unique source work recoverably. Never race the cutoff with a queued write. A timed-out test is inconclusive; a budget expiration neither approves cleanup after the deadline nor marks missing verification as passing. If stopping cannot be completed safely in the allocation, record the exact unresolved state and seek a separate cleanup authorization instead of silently continuing.

Stop production adoption for an unresolved count-rule conflict, unexplained repository effects, stale geometry application, inability to restore the semantic anchor, exceeded loading/residency bounds, competing scroll ownership, or inaccessible recovery controls. Package GREEN is not a substitute for any native gate. Failed feasibility returns a recommendation to defer zoom or propose a new interaction design rather than repeatedly extending an experiment without approval.
