# Story 12.2 Home and Search Editorial Entry Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make Home and Search feel like a calm editorial entry into reading while repairing visually tappable Home rows that currently do nothing.

**Architecture:** Home and Search adopt Story 12.1 primitives without changing repositories or input classification. Navigation remains router-owned. The Home feature receives explicit destinations for Continue, Recently Updated, and Library rows.

**Tech Stack:** SwiftUI, Swift Testing, XCUITest, existing `AppRouter`, `LibraryProviding`, and search-history services.

## Global Constraints

- Search remains the topmost and primary Home action.
- Preserve suggestion priority and URL/query classification.
- Preserve `.homeContinueReading` Browser fallback and Reader back semantics.
- Do not add promoted sources or network-backed suggestions.
- Do not change persistence schema, detection, Browser, or Reader behavior.
- Every visible row action must be functional, keyboard/VoiceOver reachable, and at least 44 pt high.
