# Story 12.6 Downloads and Settings Utilities Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace nested utility cards with focused editorial lists and accurate loading/result states in Downloads and Settings.

**Architecture:** Existing view models continue to call `CacheMetadataManaging`, `CacheStorageMeasuring`, `SettingsManaging`, and `LibraryUpdateRefreshing`. Small phase/result policies make UI states deterministic and testable. This story does not alter cache storage or update comparison logic.

**Tech Stack:** SwiftUI, Swift Testing, XCUITest, existing mock services.

## Global Constraints

- Preserve measured-storage preference and metadata estimate fallback.
- Preserve visible, descriptive, minimum-44-point removal controls.
- A failed removal keeps the row and offers retry.
- Do not describe retention metadata as guaranteed complete offline availability.
- Preserve all four Reader settings and their persistence/clamping behavior.
- Update feedback distinguishes updates, no changes, partial failure, total failure, and unavailable service.
- Do not change cache file lifecycle, update parsing/comparison, persistence schema, Browser, or Reader.
