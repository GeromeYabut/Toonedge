# Story 12.5 Library and Series Editorial Surfaces Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make Library and Series Detail content-led, borderless collection surfaces while preserving local-first readiness and exact chapter semantics.

**Architecture:** Library summaries remain lightweight; Series Detail remains locally hydrated with background refresh. Because both views currently share a 1,690-line file, first perform a behavior-preserving extraction of private Series Detail presentation into its own file, then restyle the two screens sequentially.

**Tech Stack:** SwiftUI, Swift Testing, XCUITest, existing repository/service protocols.

## Global Constraints

- Preserve Comfortable, Compact, and List density meanings and the saved preference.
- Preserve `resumeTarget` semantics; never substitute latest-known chapter as current position.
- Preserve seeded/cached instant entry, navigation-only detail readiness, one background refresh, and no schema change.
- Do not infer chapter URLs from labels or titles.
- New, Unread, In Progress, Read, update, and cache/download remain distinct concepts.
- Context-menu actions also require a visible or VoiceOver-accessible path.
- Do not change detection, Browser, Reader, update comparison, or available-chapter indexing.
