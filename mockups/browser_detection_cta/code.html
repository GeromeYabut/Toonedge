The wind howled through the obsidian pillars of the forgotten temple. Kaelen gripped the hilt of his blade, the cold metal biting into his palm. Above, the crimson moon cast a long, jagged shadow across the shattered floor. He knew they were coming. He could feel the ripple in the air before he heard the sound.

The first one emerged from the shadows like smoke taking form. It had no face, only a hollow void where features should have been. It raised a hand, and the ground began to tremble. Kaelen didn't wait. He moved with the speed of a dying star, his blade cutting a path of light through the darkness.
